Our croissants and rolls come from Moda Ekşi Maya, two streets away. The dough is made the night before; the first tray comes out at 08:00, the second at 11:00.

When they are gone, they are gone; the menu shows the next bake time next to each pastry. There are twenty [cinnamon rolls](./#urun-tarcinli-corek) a day.
