In Huila both slopes of the Andes are coffee gardens. The climate allows two harvests a year: the main one ends in April, the fly crop in October. Caturra and Castillo trees grow between 1,700 and 1,900 metres.

In the cup: red apple, caramel and milk chocolate. A bean that behaves well in filter and espresso alike; we brew it for two in the [Chemex](./#urun-chemex).
