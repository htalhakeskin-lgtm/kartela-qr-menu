Guji is in southern Ethiopia; the gardens sit between 1,950 and 2,200 metres and most are smaller than half a hectare. Growers bring their cherries to the cooperative, where the skin is removed and the seed is washed and dried.

Washed processing gives a clean cup: bergamot, peach and honey. This week the [filter of the day](./#urun-gunun-filtresi) is brewed with it, and you will find the bag under [Beans](./#urun-etiyopya-guji).
