Coffee is the seed of a fruit. It grows on slopes in the equatorial belt, between 1,100 and 2,200 metres above sea level. The higher you go, the cooler the nights, the slower the cherry ripens and the more sugar the seed stores; in the cup you taste it as bright acidity and a long finish.

That is why every filter coffee on our menu carries a number: the altitude the bean grew at. The three chapters below visit the three gardens we work with this season.
