Nyeri lies on the southern slope of Mount Kenya. Cherries reach the washing station the day they are picked; the skin comes off, the seed ferments overnight in water and is washed and dried on raised beds the next day.

This long wash gives Kenyan coffee its famous brightness: blackcurrant, grapefruit, brown sugar. Best in the [AeroPress](./#urun-aeropress), with water at 85 °C.
