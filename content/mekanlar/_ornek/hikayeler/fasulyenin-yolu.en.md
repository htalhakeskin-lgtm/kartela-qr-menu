Every autumn we buy our dried beans from the same highland village. The harvest ends in late September; the beans dry in the sun through October and reach the kitchen in November.

## Why the same village?

- The beans are small and thin-skinned, so they cook quickly after a night in water.
- Beans from the same field soften in the same time every year; no guessing at the pot.

We cook them in olive oil with very little tomato paste. If you want rice with them, try the [tradesman's plate](./#urun-esnaf-tabagi).
